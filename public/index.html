Emergency Quick Reference

Suicide / self-harm

Assess immediately, do not leave the person alone where imminent risk is suspected, escalate to senior clinical/medical staff and document the actions taken.

Violence / threat

Prioritise safety. Do not manage an acute threat alone. Use senior staff, security and emergency pathways.

Acute psychosis / severe disturbance

Seek medical/psychiatric assessment promptly. Maintain calm and avoid confrontation.

Child protection / abuse

Follow safeguarding and mandatory reporting requirements; escalate immediately and document facts.

Medical emergency

Stop counselling and activate the facility medical emergency process.

When unsure

Stay within Registered Counsellor scope. Escalate early and record who was contacted and what was decided.
