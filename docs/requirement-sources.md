| Requirement | Hours |

| Preparation & documentation of client engagement | 86 |

The SACAP workbook indicates the ethical/professional component is assessed through MySACAP and confirmed by the practicum coordinator. The Hub therefore treats it as institution/campus-led for operational monitoring. Other `site/shared` responsibility labels are programme-management classifications and can be changed if SACAP provides a more explicit responsibility split.

Counselling in the workbook includes individual/group/family counselling. The Hub therefore counts attended individual case sessions automatically and allows group/family counselling to be logged manually against the same 202-hour requirement.
